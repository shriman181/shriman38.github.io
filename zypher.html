From the founder

Built from the inside
of the problem

Before Zypher, Ashwar spent three years working at the intersection of corporate CSR and grassroots nonprofits — first as a consultant advising CSR programs, then embedded with two Tier-2 city NGOs helping them with their grant pipelines.

What he saw wasn't a funding shortage. It was an intelligence gap. Every tool available to NGOs was either too expensive for sub-₹5 Cr organisations, built for American fundraising patterns, or simply a record-keeping system dressed up as an intelligence platform.

Zypher was built to fix the specific thing that frustrates every programme officer in India: spending 40% of your week on administrative work instead of the mission you signed up for. Every feature traces back to a real conversation, a real person, a real broken workflow.
